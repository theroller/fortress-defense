# Session Journal — Fortress Defense

## Session 1 — 2026-05-04

### Finn's idea (his words)

When asked what kind of game to build, Finn said he wanted to **build something**.
When asked what kind of building was most fun, he said **defending against attacks**.
When asked what was attacking: **cannonballs**.
When asked about Ford: Finn wanted Ford to play too — **on his team**.
When asked team vs rival: Finn was torn ("on my team or a rival") and we landed on **team mode**.

### What we decided to build (v1 scope)

One fortress wall in the center of the screen. Cannonballs attack from both sides.
Finn controls the left half, Ford controls the right half.
Tapping near a broken block repairs it before the next cannonball slips through.
Health bar at the bottom — survive as many waves as you can.

### What clicked

- Finn was immediately interested when "cannonballs" came up
- The split-screen team mechanic (Finn left / Ford right) felt natural to him
- He liked that Ford gets to play but they're working together, not fighting each other

### What to build next (ideas for session 2)

- Let Finn and Ford pick their own colors / names at the start
- Add different cannonball types (faster ones, bigger ones)
- Sound effects on hit and repair
- High score / best wave tracker
